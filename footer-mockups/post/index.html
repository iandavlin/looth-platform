Post-Footer (end matter) mockups

Three layouts for the author bio + socials + related-posts carousel that follow every article. All match the header's sage palette.

  1. 1. Stacked Card — current direction, refined

    One soft container holds author bio + socials + divider + related carousel. Closest to what's already shipping; tightens spacing and adds a divider rule between author and related.

  2. 2. Split Banner — sage author / white related

    Sage-tinted card just for the author (avatar gets prominent treatment), then a clean white related section below. Emphasizes the byline.

  3. 3. Byline Strip — editorial / magazine

    Hairline-rule "Written by" treatment instead of a card. Bigger author name, more typographic. Related posts as a flat 4-col thumb grid with small uppercase label. Most magazine-feeling.

  4. 4. ★ Recommended — byline-strip + tags + share + series + related

    Option 3 plus everything I'd add: tiny top byline strip (name·date·read time·category), tags row, share buttons, conditional series-navigation card ("Read Part Two →"), then related. The full editorial close.
