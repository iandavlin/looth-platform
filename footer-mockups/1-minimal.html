Footer Option 1 — Minimal Utility

One-row footer. Brand, copyright, the smallest possible link set, social icons. Best when the site's main nav already covers everything; the footer is just legal & "we're a real org" affordance.

Maps closest to the utility-only pattern from Eleken's 2026 UX writeup. Lowest cognitive load; suits long article pages where readers shouldn't be pulled into a 40-link maze at the bottom.
