Footer mockups

Three patterns to compare for the Looth Group site footer. All share the header's sage palette and uppercase nav styling. Open each in a tab and scroll to the bottom.

  1. 1. Minimal Utility — one row

    Brand · legal links · social · ©. Smallest possible footprint. Best if the site nav already covers discovery and the footer is just legal/social affordance.

  2. 2. Columns + Newsletter — dark sage band

    Newsletter signup band on top, four-column link grid below, bottom legal bar. Doormat / sitemap-lite pattern. Highest conversion for newsletter & most "complete" feeling.

  3. 3. Editorial Big-Type — magazine voice

    Oversized brand statement + inset signup card + flowing link lists. Sage extends into the footer. Trades scannability for personality; feels like part of the editorial product.
